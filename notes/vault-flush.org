#+title: M44 — the vault on the flush pattern (midnight-examples main, Signet V1)

What upstream shipped between the M28 pin (=0d9c1660=) and main at 2026-10-07 (=375071b=, PR #91), what the re-port of the compat lineage looks like, what the harness is now, and the design of the library-side vault (rung D). The milestone itself is milestones.org M44.

* 0. What upstream changed (45 commits on the contract, 2026-09 → 2026-10)

The seventeen-circuit vault is gone. The contract is now the FLUSH PATTERN (upstream's =docs/contention-handling.md=): every action runs six steps — start, flush the request, send, queue the attestation, flush the attestation, complete — and =flushQueue= is the ONE circuit that reads or writes the two contended cells, =globalLastSeen= and =vaultAccountNonce=. A user circuit writes only entries keyed by its own request and reads configuration only the deployer changes.

Twenty-nine circuits: =initialise=, =setGasParams=, =addAllowedToken=; =flushQueue(slots: Vector<10, FlushSlot>)=; =queueAttestation0/1/32= (one per attested output width); and seven actions each with a start, a send and a complete — nonce replacement (admin, emergency), approvals (=startApproveRouter= + =startApproveStata= share =sendApprove= / =completeApprove=), deposit, withdraw, swap, supply, redeem. The =refund*= circuits are gone: the MPC's verdict (=OutputKind= executed / failed / unviable) picks the completion's arms.

A request is identified three ways along the way: the caller's =inIndex= (a =Uint<64>=, the key of =inputRequestBuffer= and the action's args map), the flush's =requestIndex= (Poseidon over the nonce and the args hash, the key of =outputRequestBuffer=, so identical requests share an index and the twin waits), and the Signet request id (the V1 preimage hash, the key of the action's event map and of =evictionMap=, which points back at the =outputRequestBuffer= index). Ownership is =ownershipCommitment(inIndex, sk)= — deliberately not =userCommitment=, so a request's owner is not linkable to a depositor identity — proven at complete over a fresh witness.

Every settle is bound to the attested block height: the flush pins =globalLastSeen= into the moved request as =lastSeen=, =queueAttestation*= verifies the signature and refuses a height at or below it, the attestation's flush raises =globalLastSeen= by a max, and =completeX= checks the record's height is strictly above the request's =lastSeen= again.

Gas is the vault's setting at start (=vaultGasLimits= per action, the two fee cells, resettable by =setGasParams=) and travels in the args; a deposit's gas is the caller's (its account signs and pays). The Signet request nonce and the caip2 cell are gone (=executionDest= is the constant =ethereumCaip2Id()=); =initialised= is a =Boolean=; =allowedTokens: Set<Bytes<20>>= gates what a deposit may bring in and what a swap may buy; =mpcKeyVersion= is a cell. =userCommitment= hashes =[sk, pad]= — the secret FIRST — where =0d9c1660= hashed the pad first.

The protocol vocabulary is the V1 one of =@sig-net/midnight= 0.24.x (midnight-integration =v0.24.1=, =a80e98e=): =SignBidirectionalEventV1= (keyVersion first, no request nonce, =respondSerializationSchema= pinned to =Bytes<0>= — an atom with no limb, 23 atoms and 30 limbs for the 2-word record), =calculateEvmType2TxParamsDigestV1= (a domain-tagged Poseidon over the scalar fields then each USED calldata word chained on), =RequestIdPreimageV1= / =calculateRequestIdV1=, =RespondBidirectionalEventV1 { requestId, blockHeight, outputKind, serializedOutputLength, digest, signature }=, =calculateSignetAttestationDigestV1= over =[tag, requestId, blockHeight, outputKind, width, output]=, =checkedTruncationU128= (byte 31 must be zero — the =Bytes<32> as Uint<128>= hazard of bug report 4).

The ledger block is 35 fields, segmented three ways by compactc (five at =[0, i]=, fifteen at =[1, i − 5]=, fifteen at =[2, i − 20]=); =#[derive(Ledger)]= computes the same paths with no change.

* 2. The V1 library (rung B, =2b3325b=)

=crates/minocrab-contracts/src/signet_v1.rs=, a sibling of =signet.rs= rather than an edit of it (the =fff3421c= shapes stay for the ports still pinned there; the tx-params structs, the ABI word helpers and the ECDSA plumbing are shared). The record is generic over the SCHEMA TYPE (=SchemaBytes<V>=, implemented by =Bytes<N>= at 31 bytes or fewer and =BytesN<V, N>= above) so one definition serves the 2-, 34-, 36- and 38-byte schemas. =EventRecordV1<WORDS, S>= is what the ledger holds; =layout= derives every offset.

Two std/ledger changes: =minocrab_std::v3::Bytes0= (compactc's =Bytes<0>=: one zero-length atom, no slot, no limb — a circuit argument and a record field), and =minocrab_ledger::impact::atom_limbs= no longer counts a zero-length atom as a limb (it had =.max(1)=; compactc's record is the witness).

* 3. The compat re-port (rung C, =2b3325b=)

=crates/minocrab-contracts/src/erc20_vault.rs=: the 29 circuits, instruction order following the Compact source, every one PI-equal to compactc's artifact. The queue machinery is four shared functions — =assert_index_free= (the =&&= short-circuit: the args-map read is guarded by the request-buffer read being false, as compactc emits it), =queue_request=, =send_request= (generic over the word count, the two schema types and the args type; the action supplies a closure building the transaction and the signing path), =settle_request= — plus the flush's two bodies. Three facts the port had to follow exactly:
- The flush's ternary arms are CONDITIONAL SCOPES: =useNext ? {…, evmNonce: vaultAccountNonce} : queued= reads the nonce cell under the guard =slotIsRequest && useNext=, the =if (useNext)= bump reads it AGAIN under the same guard, and =blockHeight > globalLastSeen ? blockHeight : globalLastSeen= re-reads =globalLastSeen= in the ELSE arm under =slotIsAttestation && !higher=. Both are =c.when(…).or_default()= reads in the port.
- =sentVaultNonce= is an if-chain over six maps whose member check and lookup are guarded per arm; =when_value … else_when … otherwise= selects the nonce out of the arm that ran.
- =deserialize<VaultResponse, 1>(output).success= is =byte == 1= with no canonicity check; the =as Uint<64>= casts inside the branches are guarded range asserts; =ownPublicKey()= is witnessed INSIDE the refund branch of =completeWithdraw= and BEFORE the branches in the three other completions.
Also: =kernel.self()= is read once per call site (no caching), as in every compat port; the identity commitment is the vault's own =user_commitment= (=[sk, pad]=), =common::commitment_transient= keeps the old order for the Pending lineage.

The harness (=tests/vault/=): =ops.rs= over 35 fields and =AlignedValue= keys (a =Uint<64>= index, a =Bytes<32>= id, a =Bytes<20>= set element); =exec.rs= with the three-segment state; =prims.rs= with the V1 hashes; =model.rs= built around =Sent= — ONE request followed from its start to its settlement (its queued entry, its flushed index, its V1 event, its id), from which the start, send, queue-attestation and complete scenarios of every action are built. =tests/erc20_vault_differential.rs=: 83 tests — per circuit a matches-corpus run (several branches each for the completions and the flush), the guard failures both artifacts must reject, and the tamper sweep with zero acceptance disagreements. The seventeen-circuit spec/adversarial harness (=gen.rs=, =spec.rs=, =erc20_vault_spec=, =erc20_vault_adversarial=) is removed; a property harness on the flush pattern is a follow-up (M44 F).

* 5. The library-side vault (rung D) — design

Ask (dmd, 2026-10-07): "make the deposit example as terse as possible using libraries". The README's side-by-side shows =deposit= from =erc20_vault_pending.rs= on the typed =Pending= API; that lineage still speaks the =fff3421c= protocol (request nonce, caip2 cell, the kind-byte attestation) and models the seventeen-circuit flows.

What the library already has that upstream's flush is a hand-written version of: the =Outbox= (M41) — a =Pending= plus a primitive =Stream= whose accumulator is =(last nonce, last seen)= under =(Add, Max)=. =call= files the pre-record and lets the ledger assign the nonce and snapshot both cells BLIND; =emit= builds the transaction at the snapshotted nonce and files the record; =complete= / =refund= are =Pending='s, then =raise_seen= checks the attested height is strictly after the snapshotted last-seen and raises last seen by a blind max. That is upstream's start + flush (one circuit, no =flushQueue=), send, and queueAttestation + flush + complete (one circuit), with no contended read anywhere — the one shared read that remains is the Signet request nonce, which the V1 protocol has removed.

Rung D therefore has three parts, in order:
1. The library to V1. =signet_flow::Signet= becomes the V1 configuration — signer, =mpcResponseKey=, =mpcKeyVersion= (a cell, so no circuit takes a key version argument), =evmChainId=; the request nonce and the caip2 cell go. =file_request= builds the V1 record (=signet_v1::construct_sign_bidirectional_event_v1=, the id by =calculate_evm_type2_request_id_v1=) with the output schema derived from the call's return type (a =Filing= names its =SchemaBytes= type and the schema string, as it names its kind today). The record map holds =EventRecordV1<WORDS, S>=. The tickets become the V1 attestation: =Succeeded<F>= / =Failed<F>= carry a =RespondBidirectionalEvent<Private>= and the serialized output at the call's width; =complete= verifies =verify_respond_bidirectional_event_v1=, asserts =outputKind == executed= and the call's success predicate; =refund= accepts failed / unviable (the digest over the EMPTY output) or executed with the predicate false (the digest over the output) — the two-preimage select the refund already does, over V1 preimages. The height is the attestation's own =blockHeight= (spec M2 has landed): =raise_seen= reads it off the event and the =HeightedTransfer= stand-in goes. =signet-sim= produces V1 attestations and reads V1 records.
2. The outbox generalised to WHO SIGNS. Upstream's deposit is signed by the depositor's derived account at the caller's nonce and gas under the depositor's path; everything else by the vault's account at the flush-assigned nonce under the vault's gas settings. =Outbox<F, Env, WORDS>= gains a signer policy: vault-signed (today's: delta =(1, 0)=, the contract path, the fee cells) or caller-signed (delta =(0, 0)=, the body carries the caller's nonce and envelope, the path is a commitment the circuit derived) — so the deposit still snapshots last seen (C3) without taking a vault nonce. The fee envelope reads the administrator's cells (=setGasParams=) instead of =Envelope::fixed()= — the seam notes/stream-design.org left open.
3. =erc20_vault_pending= re-based: the configuration and admin circuits as upstream has them, =allowedTokens=, and one =Outbox= slot per action. No =flushQueue=, no =queueAttestation*=, no =inIndex= / =outIndex= / =evictionMap=. The deposit is then three circuits — =start_deposit= (=call= with the depositor's path, nonce and gas; the environment is the depositor's commitment, the token and the amount), =send_deposit= (=emit=), =complete_deposit= (=complete= + the mint, or =refund= closing the request) — and the README's side-by-side shows =start_deposit= against upstream's =startDeposit= + =flushQueue= + =sendDeposit=.

Costed against the compat port once built (the =erc20_vault_pending= cost table, M35 C's claim re-established).

* 6. The library to V1 (rung D part 1, as built)

§5's part 1 only; parts 2 and 3 (the outbox's signer policy, the re-based =erc20_vault_pending=, the README side-by-side) are not built, and the lending-cost comparison stays =#[ignore]=d.

=signet_flow::Signet= is FOUR ledger fields — signer, =mpcResponseKey=, =mpcKeyVersion=, =evmChainId= — where V2 had six. The request-nonce counter and the caip2 cell are gone; =mpcKeyVersion= is a cell, so no request circuit takes a key version and the =keyVersion must be >= 1= guard moved to =initialize=, the one circuit that writes it (the compat vault has always carried it there). One field fewer in the block is not cosmetic: it moves the SEGMENTATION boundary, so every ledger path in the pending vault moved — =tests/vault_pending/ops.rs='s =FIELDS= is 21 and the lead segment is 6 fields rather than 7.

=file_request= makes three reads where it made five: =kernel.self()=, =evmChainId= into the transaction, =mpcKeyVersion= into the record. The caip2 read and the nonce read-modify-write both went, which is the V1 vocabulary's contention win as a count — =tests/evm_outbox.rs='s audit now asserts the stronger claim, that no shared COUNTER is read anywhere in the lineage, and that what =file_request= reads of the block is two write-once config cells.

=Filing= names an output SCHEMA (=type Schema: OutputSchema=) where it named a kind byte and a =RETURN_FIELD=. Both went: a V1 record carries no kind, and the return's NAME is inside the schema string, which rides into the record and therefore into the request id — where a =&'static str= beside it was hashed by nothing. =Filed<Call, Schema>= replaces =Kinded<Call, KIND>=; a deployment writes the impl out by hand only when two slots file the same call under the same schema and the ticket types have to stay distinct.

The settle side lost its record LOOKUP. A V2 id was a hash of the stored record, so a settle had to read those bytes back; a V1 id is a hash of =RequestIdPreimageV1= and is itself the ticket's key, so the entry's PRESENCE is the whole of what the record has to say. =Pending::consume= is member, remove, env lookup, env remove — four Impact ops fewer per settle.

The height is the attestation's own: =Outbox::raise_seen= takes the =Outcome= and reads =blockHeight= off it, so there is no way to raise last-seen past a height the MPC did not sign. =tests/evm_outbox.rs='s =HeightedTransfer= — the contrivance that decoded a transfer's return word as a height — is deleted, and the slot files a plain =erc20::Transfer= under =schema::Success=.

=signet-sim= reads and signs V1: =reader::{decode_record_v1, resolve_verified_record_v1}= (no format-version byte to check by name — what distinguishes V1 is the SHAPE, a =txParamType= at atom 4 and a five-atom tail, and the id is recomputed from the DECODED record rather than from the cell, because the transaction enters the preimage as a digest over the used words only) and =SigNetSim::respond_v1= → =AttestationV1 { request_id, block_height, output_kind, output, digest, signature, record }=. The =serializedOutput= is the EVM body alone: no kind byte glued to its front, and empty for a non-execution. =tests/signet_round_trip.rs= is green on all fifteen, including the chained =request → post → respond → settle= with the sim's own signature.

The attested WIDTH is load-bearing and the digest says so: =calculateSignetAttestationDigestV1= names the id, the height, the verdict and the LENGTH as separate field elements, then the output's FAB limbs. The Borsh lineage packed =kind ‖ output= into one byte string, which could not express a width at all — =the_typed_settle_digest_is_the_digest_the_mpc_signs= now pins the model against the MPC's rule at each of the vault's three output shapes AND that the same bytes at the wrong width are a different digest.

Costs, against the retired V2 lineage (=tests/erc20_vault_pending.rs=): every PAIR is still cheaper, and =complete_withdraw= is cheaper by 10,000 rows. The two settle-less circuits pay for V1 and get nothing back — =approve_router= 1,126 → 1,282 rows, +156 — because the record's tail carries a 34-byte schema literal where V2 carried a one-byte kind, and the id is two chained Poseidons where V2 hashed the record once. The allowance in that gate is 256 rows and says which two causes it covers; the split between them is not measured.

Gates moved in the same change: the corpus-count pins in =corpus_roundtrip= and =lean_roundtrip= (834/95 → 849/110, which rung A moved and did not update), the interface snapshot (the V1 ticket block: =requestId=, =blockHeight=, =outputKind=, =serializedOutputLength=, =digest=, the signature, then a flat =serializedOutput=), the row and leakage snapshots, and TRUST.md's table. =taint_lint= is RED on the same 20 findings rung E wrote up — not one of them moved — and stays so until dmd rules (notes/taint-lint.org §8).
